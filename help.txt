## Project goal

Create a Windows-based distribution that:

- runs smoothly on Chromebooks equipped with MrChromebox firmware  
- is as compatible as possible with Chromebook hardware  
- is optimized for low-end storage like eMMC

The two main starting points:

1. Reduce bloat and overall footprint.
2. Add and tune drivers specifically for Chromebook hardware.

## eMMC and the MCFS concept

NTFS is known to behave poorly on cheap eMMC storage—frequent random writes, stalls, and a tendency to corrupt or become unbootable on low-end devices.

Long-term, I’d like to explore a new eMMC-friendly filesystem concept:

### MCFS (MC FileSystem)

MCFS is envisioned as an NTFS-like, garbage-collected filesystem concept that:

- reads and writes in fast, controlled chunks  
- spreads writes out to avoid hammering the same eMMC cells  
- is more forgiving of inconsistency and power loss  
- aims to prevent the “boots twice then corrupts” pattern seen on some devices

This is a **research and design goal**, not a near-term promise. I will not set deadlines for MCFS; my hope is that, over time, interested filesystem and OS developers can help turn this idea into reality.
